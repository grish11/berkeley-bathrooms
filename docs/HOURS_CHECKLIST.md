# Building hours — status

**53 with hours · 69 no hours published** (122 buildings)

There is no campus-wide hours policy — most buildings never publish hours.
"No hours published" is a permanent answer, not a gap. Those buildings stay on
the map: they can still have photographed, rated bathrooms, they just show no
opening times.

Every building carries a **required** `hours_source`. A wrong time can then be
traced to where it came from, and a checked building is never researched twice.
